# Evaluation and HITL

Evaluation is deterministic and versioned. It produces a recommendation, never
an unreviewable final outcome. Risk-bearing or policy-sensitive results enter a
human review state. The reviewer sees reasons and context, records approve or
reject, and the workflow continues to signing and Document of Record generation.

Use the bead templates in `bmad/gastown/bead/templates` to keep policy changes,
review UX, integration mapping, tests, and documentation traceable.

# Incident Response Procedure

Classify incidents by confidentiality, integrity, availability, privacy, and
customer impact. Preserve correlation IDs, logs, workflow audit records, and
deployment references without copying form payloads into tickets. Assign an
incident commander, contain access or provider paths, communicate through the
approved channel, remediate, and perform a blameless review.

Exercise the procedure at least annually, including a provider outage, suspected
credential exposure, duplicate submission, and Document of Record failure.

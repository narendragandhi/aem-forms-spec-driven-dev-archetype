# Access Review Procedure

Quarterly, the system owner exports users, groups, service users, roles, tokens,
and environment permissions for Author, Publish, Dispatcher, CI/CD, Adobe Sign,
observability, and storage. The reviewer verifies business need, least privilege,
joiner/mover/leaver status, MFA where supported, and secret age. Remove stale
access, record exceptions with an expiry date, and retain reviewer sign-off.

Do not commit the export. Record only its protected evidence reference in the
evidence register and corresponding access-review bead.

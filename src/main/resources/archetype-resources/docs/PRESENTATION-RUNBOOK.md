# AEM Forms Product Presentation Runbook

Use this ten-minute structure with an experienced AEM product manager:

1. State the business journey and measurable outcome.
2. Show the authored or seeded form and an adaptive rule.
3. Submit and show the correlation/workflow identifier.
4. Show evaluation and why a human review is required.
5. Approve or reject the HITL task and explain accountability.
6. Show the signature and DoR integration seam or live evidence.
7. Open the architecture view: Author, Publish, Forms runtime, integrations,
   workflow, observability, and evidence.
8. Show archetype generation and project-specific beads.

Answer directly what is live, seeded, mocked, or blocked; how another use case
is customized; where rules/data models are versioned; how outages and duplicate
submissions are handled; and what evidence supports each production claim.
